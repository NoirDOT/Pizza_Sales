## Project Background

This project analyzes a year's worth of sales from Franco's fictitious Pizzeria, capturing the date and time of each order along with details on the pizzas served, including type, size, quantity, price, and ingredients. The goal is to assess sales performance and customer preferences by examining key metrics like product popularity, ingredient demand, peak sales times, and order patterns across categories and sizes.

Insights and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:

- **Pizza Performance**: Analyzing top- and low-performing pizzas to identify bestsellers and underperformers
- **Category and Size Preferences**: Comparing sales across categories and customer preferences for different pizza sizes
- **Peak Sales Times**: Conducting time-based analysis to pinpoint peak sales periods
- **Revenue Analysis**: Examining sales performance across different pizza categories

## Data Structure

Maven Pizza Sales database contains the following key dimensions:

| Dimension | Components |
|-----------|------------|
| **Pizza Categories** | Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie |
| **Pizza Sizes** | Small, Medium, Large, XL, XXL |
| **Sales Metrics** | Quantity sold, Total sales, Unit price |
| **Time Data** | Order dates and times throughout 2015 |

## Executive Summary

### Overview of Findings
Sales data indicates that the meat based pizza varieties like the Thai Chicken and Barbecue Chicken pizzas are the top sellers, significantly outperforming the lowest-performing Brie Carre and Green Garden pizzas which are more vegetarian inclined. Customer preferences favor ingredients like garlic, tomatoes, and red onions, with the Large size and Classic category being the most selected options. The busiest sales period is the afternoon, with Friday showing the highest weekly sales and July being the peak revenue-generating month.

### Sales Performance Insights:
- **Top-performing Pizzas**: The Thai Chicken Pizza has generated the highest sales, followed by the Barbecue Chicken Pizza and California Chicken Pizza
- **Category Performance**: Supreme category leads in revenue generation, followed by Classic category
- **Size Preferences**: Large pizza size is the most popular, followed by Medium size

### Time and Hour Insights:
- **Peak Hours**: 12 PM shows the highest sales volume, followed by 1 PM and 6 PM
- **Sales Distribution**: Lunch and dinner rushes account for the majority of daily sales

### Revenue Analysis:
- **Category Revenue**: Supreme ($380K), Classic ($360K), Chicken ($315K), Veggie ($228K)
- **Total Sales Range**: $200,000 - $380,000 across different categories

## Key Insights

![Pizza Dasboard](https://github.com/NoirDOT/Pizza_Sales/blob/main/Pizza%20Sales%20Dashboard%2001%20.PNG)

### 1. 🏆 **Pizza Performance Analysis**
The Thai Chicken Pizza dominates sales, indicating strong customer preference for Asian-inspired flavors. Barbecue Chicken and California Chicken varieties follow closely, showing the popularity of chicken-based pizzas with diverse flavor profiles.

### 2. 📊 **Category Performance Hierarchy**
Supreme category demonstrates strongest revenue performance at approximately $380,000, followed closely by Classic category at $360,000. Chicken category shows solid performance at $315,000, while Veggie category has the lowest sales at $228,000.

### 3. 📏 **Size Preference Patterns**
Large-sized pizzas dominate sales volume, suggesting customer preference for value and sharing options. Medium pizzas maintain strong performance for individual or small group orders.

### 4. ⏰ **Peak Sales Optimization**
Clear lunch (12 PM-1 PM) and dinner (6 PM-7 PM) rush patterns indicate opportunity for staffing and inventory optimization. The 12 PM hour shows peak sales volume, suggesting strong lunch business.

### 5. 💰 **Revenue Distribution**
Revenue analysis reveals consistent performance across categories with clear hierarchy. The $60,000+ gap between Supreme and Veggie categories highlights significant performance difference that could be addressed through menu optimization.

## Recommendations

### 1. **Menu Optimization Strategy**
> **Feature top-performing pizzas** like Thai Chicken and Barbecue Chicken more prominently on menus and marketing materials. Consider creating combo deals or meal bundles around these bestsellers to increase average order value.

### 2. **Peak Hour Operations**
> **Optimize staffing schedules** to align with identified peak hours (12 PM-1 PM and 6 PM-7 PM). Implement prepared dough and ingredient staging before these rush periods to improve order fulfillment speed.

### 3. **Size-Based Promotions**
> **Develop targeted promotions** for underperforming sizes during off-peak hours. Consider introducing "Lunch Special" small pizzas or "Family Deal" large pizza bundles to drive sales during slower periods.

### 4. **Category Development**
> **Expand the Supreme category** with new varieties that incorporate popular ingredients from top-performing pizzas. Consider limited-time offerings that test new flavor combinations.

### 5. **Inventory Management**
> **Align ingredient purchasing** with sales patterns of top-performing pizzas. Ensure adequate stock of chicken, cheese, and popular vegetables while optimizing inventory of less frequently used ingredients.

### 6. **Veggie Category Enhancement**
> **Revamp the Veggie category** by introducing more innovative and flavorful vegetarian options. Consider plant-based protein alternatives and globally inspired flavor profiles to attract more customers.

### 7. **Data-Driven Decision Making**
> **Implement continuous monitoring** of sales patterns and customer preferences. Use these insights to make informed decisions about menu changes, promotions, and operational improvements.
